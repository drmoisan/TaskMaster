## Summary

The delivered change satisfies every automatable acceptance criterion in `spec.md`. All 27 criteria marked `[x]` were independently re-verified this session by source inspection, direct grep over the branch diff, re-running the four toolchain stages, re-running the 69 tests in the affected namespace, and re-deriving every coverage figure from the committed JaCoCo summaries rather than accepting the executor's tabulated values.

Two verification results are worth highlighting because they were obtained independently of the executor's own evidence. First, the claim that all four new decision types reach 100% line coverage is corroborated by a package-level identity that does not depend on the per-class detail removed in commit `d0955dc4`: the `TaskMaster` package's missed-line count is identical across the baseline and final measurements while its covered count rose by exactly 186, the precise line total of the four new types. Second, the claim that the six new production files are nullable-clean was confirmed by a forced project rebuild that surfaces 195 errors the mandated incremental gate cannot see, none of them in authored code.

The three unverified criteria are unverified by design rather than by omission. AC19, AC20, and AC21 require a live Outlook process and a live mail profile; the repository's unit-test policy prohibits automated tests from depending on external processes, and this repository has no Outlook UI-automation harness. They are correctly left unchecked in `spec.md`, and the criteria themselves state that they must never be checked off on the strength of unit tests. A maintainer checklist exists and carries `Status: PENDING MAINTAINER EXECUTION`. AC21 is the only check that can prove the Office `getEnabled` callback is actually bound, since VSTO silently ignores a signature mismatch — the AC8 reflection test pins the signature but cannot prove host binding. That residual risk is materially mitigated by the defense-in-depth design: even if the callback never binds, the click guard alone makes the original defect unreachable.

No acceptance criterion is FAIL or PARTIAL, so no remediation plan is required and no `remediation-inputs` artifact is produced. The recorded findings from the code review and the policy audit are follow-up items, the most substantive being a null-conditional operator that makes the AC5-named test's own assertion vacuous when the attribute is absent; the criterion remains genuinely enforced in the meantime by the AC6 set-equality assertion.

**Recommendation: conditional GO for PR.** The single named pre-merge condition is maintainer execution of `evidence/manual-verification/ac19-ac21-checklist.2026-08-08T15-00.md` against a live Outlook profile, with the outcome recorded under `evidence/manual-verification/`. `spec.md` already names this as a pre-merge step under `## Rollout & Follow-up`.
