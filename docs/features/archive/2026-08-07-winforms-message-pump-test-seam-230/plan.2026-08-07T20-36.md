- **D13 — Factory tail asymmetry (executor-verified).** `CreateSequentialAsync` → `InitializeSequentialAsync` and `InitializeGraphicsAsync` end with fire-and-forget `_ = InitializeWebViewAsync();` and return normally under the mocked seam. `CreateAsync` → `InitializeAsync` ends with `await InitializeWebViewAsync()`, which always faults under the mocked seam (null `CoreWebView2` without the real runtime), so `CreateAsync` can never reach its `return controller;` statement in a unit test. `CreateSequentialAsync` is tested to normal completion; `CreateAsync` is tested to its controlled fault (P5-T4), mirroring the `InitializeAsync` pattern (P6-T1).
- **D14 — `Select-String` exit codes.** `Select-String` does not set `$LASTEXITCODE`; census tasks record `EXIT_CODE: 0` derived from `$?` (record the actual `$?` value if false).
- **D15 — Per-member coverage extraction for async members.** Seven of the eight de-exempted members are `async`; in Cobertura output their lines appear under compiler-generated state-machine classes (e.g. `QfcItemController+<InitializeAsync>d__NN`), not under the member name.
