It is pre-existing, outside the consolidated scope, and recorded as a follow-up.

## Detailed Review

### Production change — `TaskMaster/Ribbon/EngineToggleStateCoordinator.cs`

**Before/after shape (verified against `anchor-production-shape.md`).** Base: one `catch (` (the boundary clause at 182), zero `catch (Exception)`, `CompletePrime` with no `try`, `_logError` at 380 directly followed by `TryRemove` at 381. Head: three `catch (`, two `catch (Exception)`, `HandleToggleClickAsync` with a nested `try` inside its boundary catch (181-195), `CompletePrime` with `try` at 406, `_logError` at 408, `catch (Exception)` at 410, discard comment at 412, `TryRemove` at 415 outside the guard. Edits confined to the six documented windows (`HUNKS-OUTSIDE-WINDOWS: 0`); seven protected files byte-identical to BASE-SHA.

**Points of particular interest requested by the caller:**

1. *Broad `catch (Exception)` at two sink-guard sites versus CLAUDE.md C#4.* Both catches enclose exactly one statement — the invocation of the injected `logError` delegate — and nothing else that could throw for a different reason (the message builders are `string.Format` with `CultureInfo.CurrentCulture` over a non-null token). The boundary is clear: the sink is the type's last reporting channel, so there is no consumer to which added context could be propagated. Each site is documented twice (method `<remarks>` and the in-block comment) and names the precedent. `RibbonCommandBoundary.SafeLog` (lines 103-113) has the same shape, the same discard comment and passes the analyzer gate; the analyzer rebuild on this branch recorded `WRITESET_DIAGNOSTIC_LINES: 0`, and `RCS1075` is held at `suggestion`. Disposition: compliant as a documented boundary catch. The letter of the General Code Change Policy ("do not use broad catch-all handlers unless you immediately re-raise or propagate") is not met and is recorded as an accepted exception in the policy audit (X-1); the alternative is the defect itself.

2. *Report-then-clear ordering in `CompletePrime`.* Preserved. `_logError` (408) runs inside the `try`; `TryRemove` (415) runs after the whole `try`/`catch` statement, so it executes whether the sink returned or threw, and never before the report was attempted. The #942 contract that a caller observing the marker absent can rely on the report having been *attempted* (wording updated in E2 from "having been reported") is exactly what the code now guarantees. The `.PrimeFaultOrdering.cs` test is byte-identical and passes; `GetPressed_WhenLogSinkThrows_FirstPrimeCompletesAndMarkerIsCleared` extends the same probe to a throwing sink (`handleSeenBySink` is the still-registered marker).

3. *Click boundary never rethrows the toggle fault.* The outer `catch (Exception ex)` still terminates the method normally after the nested block; `ex` is passed to the sink and is not rethrown. `HandleToggleClickAsync_WhenToggleFaults_LogsErrorDoesNotThrowDoesNotInvalidate` (pre-existing) and the new `..._WhenLogSinkThrowsOnToggleFault_DoesNotThrowAndAttemptsReport` both pass; the latter's `BeSameAs(failure)` proves the toggle fault reaches the sink unchanged. Note the residual on the refusal path (CR-2).

4. *Containment of the discarded continuation.* `StartObservedPrime` still discards the `ContinueWith` task; with `CompletePrime` unable to throw from the sink, the only remaining throw sources inside the continuation are `completed.Exception?.GetBaseException()` and the `TaskCanceledException` constructor, neither of which throws for a faulted or canceled antecedent. The updated E3 remarks state this precisely.

**Documentation accuracy (E1-E5).** Every statement about the `catch` inventory was checked against the file: "the only `catch` clause in this type that observes an engine fault. The other two are sink guards" (154-155) and "The three `catch` clauses ... the click boundary and the two sink guards" (306-308) are both correct. Two pre-existing wording residuals remain (CR-2 remark overreach on the notify path; CR-5 "The prime task").

**Thread-safety.** Unchanged. The guards add no shared state; `_primeTasks` still has one writer (indexer under `_primeGate`) and one remover (`TryRemove` in `CompletePrime`), so the keyed removal argument from the #944 review still holds.
