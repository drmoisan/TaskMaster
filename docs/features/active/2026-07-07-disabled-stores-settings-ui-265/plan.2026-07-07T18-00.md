**F1 contract binding (from epic manifest Shared Design Alignment).** F5 is a pure consumer of F1's service exposed on `IApplicationGlobals` as the member `StoreDisable`. **Binding-seam decision (binding on Phase 3 and Phase 5).** To keep all testable controller logic free of a live `DataGridView` (the STA-construction constraint documented in research §1.3), `IDisabledStoresViewer` exposes both (a) `DataGridView Dgv { get; set; }` for the viewer's own event wiring and Designer access, and (b) a mockable binding-seam method `void BindRows(IList<DisabledStoreRow> rows)`. `DisabledStoresController.PopulateRows()` assigns its authoritative `Rows` list and calls `Viewer.BindRows(Rows)`; the concrete `DisabledStoresViewer` implements `BindRows` by assigning `Dgv.DataSource = new BindingList<DisabledStoreRow>(rows)`. The `Dgv.DataSource` write therefore lives entirely inside WinForms-exempt code, and `PopulateRows()` is verifiable with a Moq `IDisabledStoresViewer` and no live grid. Click resolution reads `Rows[e.RowIndex]` from the controller's own list, never from the grid.
