### Phase 2 — Field-Type Unblock (Seam A)

Change the `_itemViewer` field and the public constructor parameters from concrete `ItemViewer` to `IItemViewer` so `Mock<IItemViewer>` becomes injectable. The interface is NOT narrowed yet.
