## Executive Summary

The `app.config` binding-redirect corrections are mechanical, correct, and low risk: every one of the 57 corrected entries was independently re-verified against the referencing project's `.csproj` `Reference Version=` attribute, and the fix is idempotent. A minor design observation on the script's nested-function pattern and a documentation-consistency note on `issue.md`'s baseline narrative are also recorded below.
